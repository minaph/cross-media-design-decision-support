# Artifact Review

Use this reference to inspect an existing or revised artifact. Review is a
reasoned assessment of a specified version, not a generic list of preferences.

## Establish the review object

Identify:

- the artifact, version, export, commit, or dated render being reviewed;
- audience, communication job, target medium, and acceptance criteria;
- the source material and claims that the artifact represents;
- the requested review scope: content, hierarchy, implementation, rendering,
  accessibility, production, or a combination.

If the version or evidence is unavailable, report that limitation. Do not imply
that a later revision or an unstated source was reviewed.

## Gather review evidence

Inspect the artifact in the target form whenever possible. A source file alone
does not establish a print layout, projected slide, responsive page, or motion
sequence. Pair each finding with observable evidence such as a location,
rendered behavior, source record, before/after comparison, or reproducible
calculation.

For claims and data, test whether the artifact preserves:

- the intended object, action, and evidence;
- provenance and distinctions between source material, analysis, and inference;
- denominator, unit, version, calculation method, and limit for statistics;
- relevant research qualifications, including uncertainty and causal limits.

## Apply anti-patterns as diagnostic hypotheses

Read [anti-patterns.md](anti-patterns.md) when useful. First determine whether
the pattern applies to this artifact and medium. Then record the mechanism,
observable symptom, communication impact, and a proportionate correction.

Do not turn an anti-pattern label into an automatic prohibition. A card, shadow,
gradient, dense figure, or empty region may be appropriate when it has a clear
role and the rendered artifact supports that role.

## Report and revise

Order findings by impact on communication, evidence integrity, accessibility,
or delivery. For each finding, include:

- artifact location and evidence;
- why the issue matters for the stated audience and task;
- a concrete correction or an explicit decision to retain it;
- a verification method for the correction.

After revision, re-review the changed version against the accepted findings.
State which findings are resolved, which are intentionally out of scope, and
which need future evidence or a different capability. Implementation success
and communication improvement are separate conclusions.

## Medium boundary

Read [media-routing.md](media-routing.md) for medium-specific rendering checks.
Do not require state, focus, motion, playback, or interaction evidence from an
artifact that has none. Conversely, do not omit those checks where they are
part of the artifact's meaning or use.
