# Cross-media Design Decision Support

Use this skill to improve communication through design decisions before or while
producing an artifact. Start from the audience, communication job, central
message, medium, constraints, and evidence already supplied. Do not ask again
for facts that are available; state a reasonable assumption when it enables
useful progress.

## Choose a mode

- **Formative design:** select or improve content, hierarchy, space allocation,
  and a production handoff. Read [references/formative-design.md](references/formative-design.md).
- **Artifact review:** assess a named artifact version against its purpose,
  source evidence, medium, and requested criteria; then re-check the revision.
  Read [references/artifact-review.md](references/artifact-review.md).
- **Both:** form a design direction, produce or hand off the artifact, then
  review the specified version. Keep the formation and review findings distinct.

Read [references/anti-patterns.md](references/anti-patterns.md) when a design
decision or review needs a concrete failure mechanism. Read
[references/media-routing.md](references/media-routing.md) for the applicable
medium and verification. Read [references/resource-registry.md](references/resource-registry.md)
only when a known official authority, design system, asset family, or print/web
specification would inform the task. Do not load every reference by default.

## Shared constraints

- Treat cards, shadows, gradients, and other visual devices as choices with an
  information role. Do not ban them by default.
- Improve a vague claim by restoring its object, action, evidence, and limit;
  do not remove research qualifications merely to make copy shorter.
- Treat unused space as a decision: compare adding evidence, improving an
  existing explanation, converting dense material into a diagram or table, and
  retaining the space. Choose additions by value, evidence, and reading burden.
- For statistics, record the denominator, unit of analysis, artifact or dataset
  version, re-aggregation method, and generalization limit. Do not invent
  thresholds or use scores such as source coverage, item counts, or "AI-like"
  appearance as quality gates.
- Keep semantic and meaning structure through derived formats, including HTML
  versions of a source artifact. Apply interaction checks only when the artifact
  has interaction, state, or motion.
- Separate successful implementation from improved communication. Report what
  was inspected, the evidence for each conclusion, and any unverified limit.

## Capability and handoff

Use an available medium-specific skill or tool when production is requested;
do not assume one exists. If production or verification cannot be performed,
provide the completed design decisions, the missing capability, and a concrete
verification plan. A planning-only request ends with the requested decisions
and validation plan.

Do not make a particular agent, model, Git workflow, or user-approval step a
condition of this skill. Keep the user's stated authorization and scope intact.
